GSM Connect

This tab

Lines from this video or series go under this title: its own sessions, stats and review.

Help and settings
  • YouTube: turn on Japanese captions (CC).
  • Anime: load subtitles in asbplayer or Manatan. With two subtitle files in asbplayer, the first one is sent.
  • Manga: read in Manatan (or a mokuro page); each bubble is sent once when its page is on screen.
  • GSM does not have to be running: lines wait here and arrive with their original times when GSM starts.
  • Cards you make in the browser keep the browser's audio and picture; GSM adds nothing from OBS.
